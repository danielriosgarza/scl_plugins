Make the anaerobic digester of microbesGYM's control study A1 a soft_control_loop plugin. The plugin
mirrors A1's aligned experiment exactly: the same digester, lever, signals, timing, limits and score.
The user has already agreed every choice below with their assistant. Follow them as written; ask the
user only if one of them cannot be met, and say why.

Goal of the loop: maximize the digester's methane outflow over 100 days without ever leaving its
operating limits (pH at least 6.5 and total VFA at most 1.0 kgCOD/m3 at every decision). An episode
that leaves the limits has crashed and scores 0.

1. Environment. Wrap microbesGYM's A1 environment (mgym_engine.rl.GymEnv built from mgym_studies'
   A1 documents, aligned variant, reference backend); do not copy or change it. The context file
   gives the exact calls. The environment class takes no required keyword arguments.

2. Lever. The action is a list with one float, the controlled pump's feed in m3/d: action_space is
   Box([0.0], [255.0]). The wrapper converts it to the experiment's normalized action. The pump starts
   every episode at 85 m3/d, and the engine moves it at most 34 m3/d towards the commanded feed at
   each decision. The plant's own influent arrives on a fixed schedule that is the same in every
   episode; its composition is drawn at random per episode and is never observed.

3. Timing. A decision every 6 hours, 400 decisions (100 days). The episode ends early only through
   A1's own termination (the digester soured); otherwise it is truncated after decision 400.

4. Observation (a dict). day: the decision number times 0.25 (0 at the reset). For each of the eight
   signals ch4_flow and co2_flow (gas meter, m3/d), ch4_pressure (methane partial pressure, bar),
   vfa_s_va, vfa_s_bu, vfa_s_pro and vfa_s_ac (valerate, butyrate, propionate and acetate assays,
   kgCOD/m3, which is the experiment's kgCOD/L times 1000) and inorganic_carbon (assay, mM): the
   newest reading, decoded as the context file shows, and 0.0 while the signal has no value yet.
   assay_age: the number of decisions since the newest assay values arrived, or -1 before the first.
   The gas signals arrive at every decision, so they need no age. The five assays arrive together
   every fourth decision, each sampled four decisions before it arrives, so the first ones arrive
   at decision 4 and share one age. Readings carry A1's measurement noise. A program sees nothing
   else: no true state and no influent composition.

5. Reward. At each decision the reward is the true methane outflow (m3/d) after the step, read from
   the true state as A1's own evaluation reads it. It is not the engine's reward.

6. Episode record. The observation also has the keys mean_ch4_flow, min_ph, max_total_vfa,
   first_out and score. They are None at every step and are filled in only in the observation that
   ends the episode, which no program acts on: the mean true methane outflow over the decisions,
   the lowest true pH, the highest true total VFA (kgCOD/m3), the number (from 1) of the first
   decision outside the limits or None, and A1's score (the mean true methane outflow if first_out
   is None, else 0). Checks read them from trace.final_observation.

7. Seeds. Debug 1001 and 1002; evaluation 5001 to 5010; baseline 6001, 6002 and 6003. Seeds 1001 to
   1008 and 6001 to 6020 use A1's training draws, every other seed its validation draws; every
   episode runs in stochastic mode.

8. Limits and budgets. max_steps 400; timeout 600 seconds per episode (an episode takes about
   30 seconds); step_deadline null; pass_rate 1.0, because an episode that leaves the limits scores
   0; explore 3; budgets: dollars 6, attempts 15, episodes 10.

9. Baseline conditions, each a program that ignores the observation: hold_0, hold_64, hold_128,
   hold_192 and hold_255 command that feed (m3/d) at every decision; step_64_192 commands 64 for
   decisions 0 to 199 and 192 from decision 200 (day 50).

10. First milestones. within_limits: the episode ran all 400 decisions and never left the limits
    (first_out is None). within_limits_2000: within_limits, and mean_ch4_flow is at least 2000 m3/d.
    Propose subgoals and strategies for each from the baseline traces.

11. The description in setup.json must tell the coordinator, in these words or close to them:
    "A1's published results (its baseline ladder, tuned controllers, learned agents and reports) are
    not targets. Do not use them for the goal or any milestone. Set each new milestone above what
    this loop's own best program reaches, and keep raising it: the goal is the most methane within
    the limits, not beating a reference."
